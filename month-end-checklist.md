# Month-end checklist: salon or spa

Work top to bottom. The card deposits come first because every split after
them (services, retail, tips) starts from those numbers.

Month: ____________  Closed by: ____________  Date locked: ____________

## 1. Get everything in
- [ ] Bank and card statements for the full month, as PDFs
- [ ] Card processor monthly statement (gross volume, fees, deposits)
- [ ] Booking system sales summary: services, retail, tips, gift cards sold
      and redeemed
- [ ] Payroll report for every pay run in the month
- [ ] Distributor invoices, with retail and backbar lines marked

## 2. Pull the card deposits apart
- [ ] Each deposit grossed back up: services + retail + tips = card volume
- [ ] Card volume - processor fee = the amount that reached the bank
- [ ] Processor fees booked as merchant fees, not netted out of sales
- [ ] Fee rate for the month worked out (fees / volume) and compared with last
      month

## 3. Tips
- [ ] Card tips collected went to tips payable, not revenue
- [ ] Tips paid out on payroll came out of tips payable
- [ ] Tips payable is 0.00 after the last payroll, or the leftover is tips
      still owed and listed by stylist

## 4. Cash
- [ ] Cash services from the booking system match the cash deposits
- [ ] Any cash kept for the drawer or paid out is written down

## 5. Booth rent
- [ ] Rent received from each renter booked as booth and room rent income
- [ ] Any renter behind on rent listed

## 6. Retail and backbar
- [ ] Each distributor invoice split: product for resale to cost of retail
      products, color and supplies to salon supplies
- [ ] Retail margin worked out: retail sales - cost of retail products

## 7. Gift cards and packages
- [ ] Gift cards and packages sold added to the liability
- [ ] Redemptions taken out of the liability and into revenue
- [ ] Liability balance agrees with the booking system's outstanding gift card
      report

## 8. Payroll from the report
- [ ] Gross wages and commissions, employer taxes and withholdings booked from
      the payroll report, not the net pay leaving the bank

## 9. Reconcile and review
- [ ] Every bank and card account ties to its printed closing balance
- [ ] Sales tax payable = tax collected on taxable sales - tax remitted
- [ ] Profit and loss compared with last month; any big move explained
- [ ] Uncategorized at zero

## 10. Lock and send
- [ ] Month locked so a late edit can't change it
- [ ] Reports sent to the owner, with open questions listed
